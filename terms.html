Terms of use

The short, plain-English version.

What Charge is

Charge is a free school planner made by a high school student. It's offered as-is, while it's still being built, so things may change or break.

Your stuff

  • Your plans are yours. They're saved on your device, not on our servers. If you clear your browser or lose your phone, they can be lost, so use Send to my other device or Backup in Settings.
  • Ask Charge and the AI planner send the question you type and a short summary of your day to an AI service to get an answer. Don't type passwords or private information into it.

Using it well

  • Charge is a planning helper, not a teacher, doctor or counsellor. Always check due dates with your teacher or school portal.
  • Energy points and sleep tips are rough guides, not medical advice. If you're feeling exhausted or unwell a lot, talk to a parent, a school counsellor or a doctor.
  • AI answers can be wrong. Double-check anything important.
  • Don't try to break, overload or misuse Charge or its AI.

Age

Charge is made for high school students. If you're under 13, use it with a parent's permission.

Changes

These terms may be updated as Charge grows. The newest version is always on this page.
