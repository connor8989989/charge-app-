Charge privacy

Last updated: November 3, 2026.

The short version

Charge does not need an account. Your plans stay on your device unless you use the AI features described below. Everything you type (your name, school, subjects, tests, homework, routines and energy ratings) is saved only in the browser on your device.

What Charge stores

Your planner data is kept in your browser's local storage on your phone or computer. Charge does not collect, sell or share it, and the people who make Charge cannot see it.

What leaves your device

When the app loads it downloads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google can see that a device loaded the font files. Charge does not use ads, analytics or tracking.

Ask Charge and the AI planner

When you use Ask Charge, the AI planner or Quiz me, Charge sends what you typed plus a short summary of your day (your first name, grade, subjects, today's schedule and upcoming tests) to Charge's AI server, which uses Cloudflare's AI to write an answer. Charge's server doesn't save it. If you don't use these features, nothing is sent. Don't type passwords or private information into the chat.

Counting visits

Once a day, Charge sends a random code for your device (just letters and numbers, made up on your phone) so we can count how many people use Charge each day. It doesn't include your name, email, school or plans, and the code can't be traced back to you. We only keep the daily totals.

Moving to another device

"Send to my other device" puts your plans inside the link itself, after the # sign. Browsers never send that part of a link to any server, including ours. Anyone you send the link to could open your plans, so only send it to yourself.

If you sign in (optional)

You can use Charge without signing in. If "Save to an account" is turned on in Settings (it is not available yet), Charge uses your email address only to send you a sign-in link and to keep your plans in your own account, so they work on any device you sign in on. There is no password. Your plans are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and the database only lets a signed-in person read or change their own plans; nobody else using Charge can see them. We never sell your email, show it to other users, or use it for ads or newsletters. To delete your account and everything in it, email the contact address below.

Backups

If you use Backup in Settings, Charge makes a file on your device. Keep it somewhere safe; anyone with that file can read your planner.

Deleting your data

Use "Clear data" in Settings, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Uninstalling the app from your home screen also removes it on most phones.

Students under 13

Charge is designed for high school students. Because Charge doesn't collect personal information, it can be used without an account, but younger students should use it with a parent's permission.
