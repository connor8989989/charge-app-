Charge privacy

Last updated: [DATE]. Have a parent or trusted adult review this page before you share Charge publicly.

The short version

Charge does not have accounts and does not send your plans anywhere. Everything you type (your name, school, subjects, tests, homework, routines and energy ratings) is saved only in the browser on your device.

What Charge stores

Your planner data is kept in your browser's local storage on your phone or computer. Charge does not collect, sell or share it, and the people who make Charge cannot see it.

What leaves your device

When the app loads it downloads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google can see that a device loaded the font files. Charge does not use ads, analytics or tracking.

If you sign in (optional)

You can use Charge without signing in. If you choose "Save to an account" in Settings, Charge uses your email address only to send you a sign-in link and to keep your plans in your own account, so they work on any device you sign in on. There is no password. Your plans are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and the database only lets a signed-in person read or change their own plans; nobody else using Charge can see them. We never sell your email, show it to other users, or use it for ads or newsletters. To delete your account and everything in it, email the contact address below.

Backups

If you use Backup in Settings, Charge makes a file on your device. Keep it somewhere safe; anyone with that file can read your planner.

Deleting your data

Use "Clear data" in Settings, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Uninstalling the app from your home screen also removes it on most phones.

Students under 13

Charge is designed for high school students. Because Charge doesn't collect personal information, it can be used without an account, but younger students should use it with a parent's permission.
